### Long description (250 words)
VanGuard is a food safety compliance app built exclusively for UK mobile caterers — food trucks, street food traders, catering vans and market stall operators.

Most mobile caterers keep food safety records on paper log books that get wet, lost or forgotten. VanGuard puts everything on your phone: fridge and freezer temperature logs, cleaning records, opening and closing checklists — all with automatic pass/fail alerts and daily push notification reminders.

When an Environmental Health Officer (EHO) shows up unannounced, you hand them your phone or export a clean, dated PDF showing months of organised records. This demonstrates due diligence under UK food safety law and can directly improve your Food Hygiene Rating.

Key features:
- Temperature logging for unlimited appliances (fridges, freezers, hot-hold)
- Cleaning schedule checklists with sign-off
- Custom opening and closing checks
- PDF export for EHO inspections
- Full offline support — works with no signal
- Daily reminder push notifications
- 14-day free trial, no card required

VanGuard is a progressive web app (PWA) — no App Store required. Visit vanguardapp.co.uk in your browser and tap "Add to Home Screen."

Pricing: £7.99/month or £99 one-off lifetime access.

## REFERRAL SCHEME — HOW IT WORKS (for your reference)

- Each user gets a unique referral link: vanguardapp.co.uk?ref=XXXXXXXX
- When someone signs up via that link, their ref code is stored
- When they subscribe, manually apply a free month to both accounts via Stripe dashboard (Customers → Add credit)
- Future: automate with a Stripe coupon applied at checkout

The ref code is now tracked in the app and stored in user metadata at sign-up — ready for manual or automated fulfilment.
