Founder story
"I built the app
I wish existed
when I started trading."
I kept seeing the same thing in street food groups — caterers stressing about EHO visits, losing paper records, scrambling to fill in backdated logs the night before an inspection.

So I spent a year building VanGuard. Temperature logs, checklists, cleaning schedules, LPG records — all on your phone. And one tap generates a full EHO audit PDF.

No download. No monthly faff. Just tap and go.
