# VanGuard — Distribution & Backlinks Playbook

Goal: earn backlinks and referral traffic that (a) help you rank in Google and (b) put VanGuard in front of mobile caterers. Work top-down — the earlier items are higher-leverage.

**Key distinction:** VanGuard is *software sold to caterers*, not a caterer for hire. So directories like foodtruck.uk, PopTop and Food Marketplace are **lead sources** (places to *find* caterers to contact) — not places to list VanGuard. List VanGuard in general, SaaS, and supplier directories instead.

---

## 0. First: lock your "business identity" (do this once)

Use the **exact same** details everywhere you list. Consistency (called NAP — Name, Address, Phone) is what makes citations count for local SEO. Inconsistent details cancel each other out.

Copy-paste block — fill the blanks, then reuse verbatim:

- **Business name:** VanGuard
- **Website:** https://vanguardapp.co.uk
- **Email:** hello@vanguardapp.co.uk
- **Phone:** __________ (a number you'll keep — even a mobile)
- **Area served:** United Kingdom (nationwide)
- **Category:** Software company / Business management software
- **Short description (155 chars):**
  > Food safety app for UK mobile caterers. Log fridge temps, cleaning & van checks, and generate an EHO-ready report in one tap. 14-day free trial, no card.
- **Long description:**
  > VanGuard is a food safety compliance app built for UK mobile caterers — food trucks, trailers, coffee vans and event caterers. Log temperature checks, cleaning schedules and van/equipment checks in seconds, and produce a full EHO-ready inspection report with one tap. Works on any phone, nothing to install. 14-day free trial, no card required, then £7.99/month.

## 1. Google Business Profile — biggest single win (do first)

Free, high-authority, and shows you in Google Maps/Search. Set up as a **service-area business** (no storefront, address hidden).

1. Go to **google.com/business** → create profile for "VanGuard".
2. When asked "Do you have a location customers can visit?" → **No**.
3. Set **service area** = United Kingdom (or your key cities/regions — up to 20).
4. Category: **Software company** (add "Business management software" as secondary).
5. Add website, email, hours, logo (`icon-512.png`) and 3–4 screenshots.
6. Verify (postcard/phone/email — follow Google's prompt).
7. Post your guides as "Updates" now and then — free reach.

> Tip: because you have no shopfront, Google *requires* you to hide the address — that's expected for a service-area business.

## 4. Catering-industry / niche relevance

Links from food-safety and catering contexts are more topically relevant than generic ones — Google weights that.

- **NCASS** (ncass.org.uk) — the Nationwide Caterers Association. Not a place to "list" as a caterer, but explore their **supplier/partner** side: a business that helps caterers stay compliant is a natural fit for a partnership, guest article, or supplier mention. Highest-value relationship on this list — worth an email.
- **Catering & street-food forums / blogs** — offer to write a guest guide (reuse your EHO checklist content) with a link back.
- **Local council / food-safety business pages** — some publish "helpful resources for food businesses." A polite email suggesting your free guides can earn a link.

## 5. Community distribution (drives traffic + can earn links)

This is where mobile caterers actually hang out. **Share the guides, don't pitch the app** — lead with help, link the guide, let it do the selling.

Where:
- Facebook groups: search "UK street food traders", "mobile catering UK", "burger van owners UK", "catering trailer", "food truck UK". Join 4–5 active ones.
- Reddit: r/StreetFoodUK, r/foodtrucks, r/CasualUK (when relevant), r/smallbusinessuk.
- Any WhatsApp/Discord trader groups you're in.

Ready-to-paste posts (adjust tone to each group's rules — some ban links, so post value first and drop the link in a comment):

> **Post A (EHO angle):**
> Put together a plain-English EHO inspection checklist for mobile caterers — what officers actually check and the records to have ready. Free, no sign-up: https://vanguardapp.co.uk/eho-inspection-checklist-mobile-caterers — hope it's useful before someone's next visit.

> **Post B (temperatures angle):**
> Made a quick reference for safe food temperatures + what to record for a food van (fridge, hot-hold, cooking, cooling). Free guide here: https://vanguardapp.co.uk/fridge-temperature-records-food-truck

## Suggested order & cadence

- **Today:** Google Business Profile + Bing Places (30 min).
- **This week:** Capterra/GetApp + SaaSHub + AlternativeTo; 5 general directories.
- **Ongoing (1–2/week):** one community post sharing a guide; email NCASS about a supplier/partner angle.
- **Track it:** keep a simple list of where you've listed (avoid duplicates) and watch Search Console → Links and GA referrals over the next few weeks.

Don't mass-submit to hundreds of low-quality "SEO directory" sites — a handful of relevant, real listings beats 200 spammy ones, which can even hurt you.
