# VanGuard Clean — International Starter Template Pack

These templates are operational starting points. Customers must adapt them to their contract scope, premises and applicable local requirements.

## INTL — Office Daily Cleaning

- Empty waste bins and replace liners
- Vacuum/carpet-clean floor areas
- Sweep/mop hard floors
- Clean and disinfect high-touch surfaces
- Clean internal glass and mirrors
- Clean washrooms and sanitary fixtures
- Check washroom consumables
- Clean staff/kitchen areas
- Check entrances and reception areas
- Record defects, hazards or missing supplies

## INTL — Restroom Inspection

- Toilets/urinals visibly clean
- Basins and taps clean
- Mirrors clean
- Floors clean and dry
- Touch points cleaned
- Soap available
- Paper/towels available
- Waste bins emptied
- Odour acceptable
- Defects or maintenance issues recorded

## INTL — Site Safety Walk

- Floors/walkways free from obvious trip hazards
- Wet-floor controls/signage used where needed
- Cleaning equipment appears safe to use
- Chemicals are labelled and stored appropriately
- Required PPE is available for the task
- Access/egress routes remain clear
- Defects or hazards recorded with evidence
- Corrective action assigned where required

## US variant

Use the same operational tasks, with optional prompts for chemical/SDS checks and hazard communication records. Do not label a checklist as an OSHA certification. OSHA's cleaning-industry guidance covers hazards including chemicals, falls, PPE and ergonomics, and state-plan requirements can differ.

## UK variant

Use the same operational tasks, with optional prompts for COSHH-related controls and workplace risk observations. Do not label a checklist as HSE certification.

## Canada / Australia

Use the international operational templates and add jurisdiction-specific prompts only after validating the applicable provincial/state/territory requirements.
