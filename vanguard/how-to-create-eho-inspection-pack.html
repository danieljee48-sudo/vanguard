How to create an EHO inspection pack from your records

When an EHO visits, handing over a complete records pack sets the tone. Here is how to pull your records into one report you can produce on the spot.

Free: the one-page inspection checklist (PDF)Print it, stick it in the van. No sign-up, no email needed — download the checklist.

What an inspection pack should contain

  • Temperature logs (fridge, freezer, hot-hold, cooking)
  • Daily diary (opening and closing checks)
  • Cleaning schedule and sign-offs
  • Equipment and LPG safety checks
  • Water and pitch records

How to produce it

  1. Choose the period you want to show (today, this week, this month, or all time).
  2. Export your records to a single PDF.
  3. Print it or save as PDF, and hand it to the inspector at the start of the visit.
Handing an inspector a complete, organised pack up front creates a strong first impression and often reduces the time they spend on site.

Paper or digital?

Digital records are accepted and often preferred — they're time-stamped, legible and can't get wet or lost. The key is that they're accurate and available the moment an officer asks.
