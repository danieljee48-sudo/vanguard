What is VanGuard?

VanGuard is a mobile catering app for food safety — a mobile app for catering businesses that replaces paper log books with a simple app on your phone. Every morning you log your temperatures, tick off your opening checks, and record your cleaning — it takes about 60 seconds.

When an EHO visits, you hand them your phone and show them months of clean, organised records. No scrambling through paper. No "I left it in the van."

Why Not Just Use Paper?

Paper log books work, but they have real problems for mobile caterers:

  • They get wet, lost or left in the van
  • You can't access them on your phone when an EHO calls
  • They don't alert you when a temperature fails
  • You have to remember to do them — no reminders
  • You can't search back through months of records quickly

VanGuard solves all of these. Your records are always on your phone, always backed up, and always ready to show.

Does It Meet UK Food Safety Requirements?

Yes. VanGuard is designed around UK food safety law requirements — specifically Regulation (EC) No 852/2004 and the Food Safety Act 1990. The app captures the key records that EHOs look for during routine inspections: temperature monitoring, cleaning schedules, and due diligence checks.

The export feature produces a clear, dated PDF that satisfies the "due diligence" defence — showing your EHO that you have robust food safety management in place. This can directly improve your Food Hygiene Rating.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install an app from the App Store?

No. VanGuard is a web app — you visit vanguardapp.co.uk in your browser and tap "Add to Home Screen" to install it. It works like a native app but without the App Store.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. VanGuard works offline. You can log temperatures and checks with no signal, and everything syncs automatically when you're back online.

Is my data safe?

Yes. VanGuard uses Supabase (enterprise-grade PostgreSQL) to store your records securely. Your data is backed up automatically and never shared with third parties.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts. Cancel your subscription at any time from your account screen.

What if I have multiple appliances?

No problem — you can add as many fridges, freezers and hot-hold units as you need, each with their own temperature thresholds.
