# VanGuard — User Acquisition Playbook

**Core principle:** caterers buy compliance tools on **trust and peer proof**, not interruption ads. Meta failed (0/100) because it's cold + untargetable for this niche. The whole play is: **get in front of real caterers through channels they already trust, lead with value, let peers vouch.** SEO compounds underneath; paid is a small intent-based supplement only *after* warm traffic proves conversion.

**Non-negotiable: measure everything.** Every link gets a UTM tag or unique code (`?utm_source=ncass`, `?ref=creatorname`) so we know which channel/creator actually drives trials. The Meta lesson: never spend blind again.

**Messaging spine (use everywhere):** the fear trigger. *"Don't lose your hygiene rating to missing paperwork. Log your checks in a minute a day and hand the EHO a full report in one tap."* Fear of failing an inspection is what sells this.

## TIER 1 — Free, targeted, start now (this is the engine)
1. **Community presence.** Founder-led posting in UK street-food / mobile-catering **Facebook groups** + **NCASS forums**: answer food-safety questions, share the free checklist, be the helpful expert. Soft app mention. ~20–30 min/day. See `FACEBOOK_GROUP_POSTS.md`.
2. **Direct outreach.** Hand-picked **Instagram + FB DMs** to active caterers (warm-up → free checklist → trial). Perfect targeting, £0. See `INSTAGRAM_OUTREACH.md` + `TRIAL_DM_SCRIPT.md`.
3. **NCASS (Nationwide Caterers Association) — biggest single lever.** Thousands of exactly-right members. Approaches, in order of effort: contribute a food-safety article to their newsletter/blog → get listed in member resources/marketplace → formal partnership (member discount / affiliate). One good NCASS placement > months of ads.

## TIER 2 — Force multipliers
4. **Niche micro-creators (the "influencer" play — see below).** UK food-truck / street-food / catering-business creators whose audience *is* caterers.
5. **Adjacent-business partnerships.** Businesses that already sell to caterers and can recommend/bundle VanGuard for an affiliate cut: **catering-trailer builders/sellers, commissary & ghost kitchens, catering-equipment suppliers, food-hygiene (Level 2/3) course providers, mobile-catering insurers.** High trust — they own the customer relationship. A simple "recommend us, get 20% of the first year" affiliate deal.
6. **SEO (already built ~20 articles).** Slow burn, compounds. Keep publishing; not the 90-day engine.

## TIER 3 — Paid, only once warm traffic converts
7. **Google Search** ("food safety app mobile catering", "EHO record app") — *intent*, not interruption. Tiny budget, low volume, but real.
8. **Meta LEAD-GEN for the free checklist** (never cold app-signup). Value-first: capture real caterers' emails, nurture to trial. Only if you insist on Meta.

## The influencer sub-play (answering "is a paid influencer an option?")
**Yes — and it's probably your best *paid* channel — but only the right kind.**

- **DO target niche micro-creators (5k–50k followers):** food-truck build channels, "day in the life of a burger van", street-food vloggers, catering-business coaches on TikTok/IG/YouTube. Their audience is aspiring + active caterers = your buyer.
- **DON'T pay big generic "foodie" influencers** — their audience is *diners*, not caterers. That's Meta's mistake in human form.
- **Best format:** an authentic *"how I stay EHO-ready"* segment where the creator genuinely uses the app — ideally a creator who's an actual caterer. Authentic use > scripted ad-read.
- **Deal structure:** start with **1–2 creators**, £50–200 each (or gift the app + affiliate commission + a small fee). Give each a **unique link/code** so trials are attributable. Scale only what tracks.
- **Ask:** "Would you show your followers how you keep your food-safety records? I'll set you up free for life + [£X / 20% affiliate]. Just an honest take — if it's not useful, say so."
- **Why it beats Meta:** targeted (their audience = caterers), trust-transfer (peer proof), and the content keeps working after you pay once.

## Kickstart the trust flywheel: "Founding Members"
Offer the first ~20 caterers **founding-member status**: extended free trial or lifetime discount, in exchange for feedback + (if they love it) a testimonial/referral. Reviews + logos + word-of-mouth → social proof → the next signups get easier. Solves the cold-start trust problem money can't.

## 30-day sequence
- **Wk 1:** Community posting live (2–3 groups) + 10–15 IG DMs/day. Approach NCASS (newsletter article pitch). Shortlist 5 niche creators. Add UTM tracking to all links.
- **Wk 2:** First trials in → run founding-member offer + feedback loop. Line up 1–2 creators. Rook ships top-3 UI fixes from feedback.
- **Wk 3:** Creator content goes live (tracked). Approach 3 adjacent partners (trailer builders / hygiene-course providers). Keep community cadence.
- **Wk 4:** Double down on whatever channel produced trials; drop what didn't. First paid conversions from early trials ending.

## KPIs (track weekly, by source)
Visitors → trials started → **activated** (logged ≥1 record) → paid. If trials come but activation is low → onboarding UI. If a channel sends visitors but no trials → wrong audience, cut it.
