# Instagram Outreach — mobile caterers

**Why IG works here:** caterers live on Instagram — they post their food, their pitch, their events. It's the best channel for *finding specific active traders* and starting a warm 1:1. Better than cold email for this niche.

## Find them
Search these hashtags and tap "Recent" (active accounts, not big brands):
`#streetfooduk` `#foodtruckuk` `#mobilecatering` `#cateringtrailer` `#burgervanuk` `#coffeevanuk` `#streetfoodtrader` `#horseboxbar` `#pizzavanuk`
Also **location tags** for UK food markets / festivals / "[town] street food". Prioritise accounts that: post regularly, are clearly a real trading business, UK-based, small (not a chain).

## The warm-up (do NOT cold-DM out of nowhere)
For each target, before messaging:
1. Follow them.
2. Like 2–3 recent posts.
3. Leave ONE genuine comment (about their food, not your app).
Then DM a day later. This 10× the reply rate and keeps you off Instagram's spam radar.

## Safety limits (important — don't get the account restricted)
- **10–20 personalised DMs/day max**, spaced out. IG restricts accounts that blast identical messages.
- **Vary the wording every time** — never paste the exact same text.
- **No link in the first DM** to someone who doesn't follow you (IG buries link-DMs from strangers and it reads as spam). Offer the checklist; send the link once they reply.
- DMs to non-followers land in their "Requests" folder — that's normal.

## IG DM script (casual, references their content)
**Opener (personalised):**
> Hey! Love what you're doing with [their thing — e.g. "the birria tacos"] 🌮 Quick one — are you still keeping your food-safety records on paper? I made a free one-page EHO checklist for mobile caterers (the stuff inspectors actually check). Want me to send it over? No catch.

**When they reply "yes":**
> Here you go 👇 [checklist PDF/link]
> Full honesty: I also built an app that does the whole thing on your phone — temp logs, reminders, EHO-ready PDF. Free for 30 days if you fancy trying it. I'm after honest feedback more than anything — would you tell me what's confusing about it? vanguardapp.co.uk

**Then follow the feedback/convert/referral scripts in `TRIAL_DM_SCRIPT.md`.**
