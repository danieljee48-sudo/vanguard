# VanGuard — Paid Influencer/Partner Outreach (trade-facing, £50–200/post)

**Scope of this doc:** you asked for influencers to *pay* to promote VanGuard, focused on reaching mobile caterers/street food traders directly (not the general public), at roughly £50–200 per post. Findings below are from live research + browsing today (10 Aug 2026) — follower counts are correct as of today but will drift, so re-check before you actually message anyone.

## Reality check first
UK mobile catering/street food doesn't have a mature "influencer" industry the way beauty or fitness does — there's no ready directory of accounts with big *trade-only* audiences, and searching hashtags like `#foodtruckuk` / `#mobilecateringuk` mostly surfaces individual trucks posting their own menus and pitches (i.e. potential **customers**, not people with an audience of other traders to sell to). At your budget, the two things that actually work are:
1. **Trade organisations/media** who already have thousands of caterers following them — pay for a mention, not a full "campaign."
2. **A handful of individual trader-operators** with modest but real followings who post about *running the business* (van builds, kit, day-in-the-life) rather than just food — that content is what other caterers actually watch. £50–200 + free VanGuard access is a fair, well-received offer for this tier.

## Tier 1 — Trade organisations & media (highest relevance — contact this week)

**British Street Food Awards** — [@britishstreetfoodawards](https://www.instagram.com/britishstreetfoodawards/) — 17.7K followers, verified today
Run the UK's biggest street food competition (regional heats through Aug–Sept 2026, finals later in the year). Nearly all followers are traders entering or watching the competition — about as close to a pure trade audience as this niche gets. At £50–200 realistically buys a shout-out Story or a mention in a heats post, not a "finals sponsor" slot. Contact via linktr.ee/britishstreetfood or the britishstreetfood.co.uk editorial team — they also run a blog, so a guest article ("EHO checklist for competition traders") + a paid mention could be pitched as one package.

**NCASS / The Association for Independent Hospitality** — [@ncass_uk](https://www.instagram.com/ncass_uk/) — 8.5K followers, verified today
The UK trade association for independent caterers — literally VanGuard's customer base. This isn't a classic "influencer" so don't pitch it as one: propose sponsoring a piece of their content, a member discount/offer, or a guest guide, rather than a flat per-post fee. This was already flagged as the highest-value relationship on this list in `VanGuard-distribution-playbook.md` — worth folding this ask into that outreach rather than running it separately.

## Tier 2 — Regional trader collectives (cheap or free, small but 100% relevant)

**Cambridge Food Truck Collective** — [@cambridgefoodtrucks](https://www.instagram.com/cambridgefoodtrucks/) — 528 followers, "Cambridge's first trader-run independent street food collective," verified today. Small reach but exact ICP.

Similar volunteer/trader-run collectives exist in other UK cities — search Instagram for "[city] street food collective," "[city] food truck association," or "[city] street food traders" (Leeds, Bristol, Manchester and Brighton are worth checking). These groups aren't commercial influencers, so a small flat fee (£20–50) or simply free VanGuard access in exchange for a shout-out to members is usually enough — fits your existing non-paid-ads instinct better than a full "influencer deal."

## Tier 3 — Individual trader-operators (the real "nano-influencers" here)

No pre-built list of these exists — I looked, and the honest answer is you have to scout live, the same way `growth/INSTAGRAM_OUTREACH.md` already describes for finding trial users. The difference for a *paid promo* ask is the filter: instead of any active trader, look for accounts that post regularly about **running the business** — van builds, kit reviews, pricing, "day in the life," EHO visits — rather than just food photography. That's the content other caterers actually follow for advice, which is what makes a paid mention credible.

Target size: **2,000–15,000 followers.** Big enough that a post reaches a real number of peers; small enough that £50–200 is a fair, appreciated fee rather than an insult.

Where to look: the same hashtags as before (`#streetfooduk` `#foodtruckuk` `#mobilecatering` `#cateringtrailer` `#burgervanuk` `#coffeevanuk` `#streetfoodtrader` `#horseboxbar` `#pizzavanuk`), plus check who British Street Food Awards and NCASS are followed by / follow back — that overlap is a good shortlist of respected, trade-visible accounts. I'd rather hand you 5–10 *verified* current candidates than guess handles from a hashtag scroll and get it wrong — happy to do that scouting pass with you live (I have browser access) whenever you want to spend 20–30 minutes on it.

## Bonus precedent worth knowing about
NCASS's own street-food-finder partner, **My Streats**, brought on **The Fat Foodie** (a consumer street food reviewer) to visit markets, talk to vendors on camera, and spread the word about the app to traders and customers alike. That's a different model — a bigger consumer-facing reviewer lending credibility at markets rather than a trade-only account — and doesn't fit your "trade-facing" choice for this round, but it's a proven playbook if you want a visibility push later (Tier "consumer" from the options I gave you).

## Paid-partnership outreach script
Different from the free-trial DM script in `TRIAL_DM_SCRIPT.md` — this one's explicitly offering payment, so lead with that up front rather than a soft warm-up.

> Hey [name] — I run VanGuard, a UK app that does food-safety records (temps, cleaning, EHO reports) for mobile caterers. Really like [something specific about their content/business]. I'd like to pay you £[X] for a Story or post about it — free lifetime access either way, no obligation to say anything you don't mean. Happy to jump on a call if easier, or just send you a trial to try first. Interested?

Keep the same safety limits as the trial-DM approach: personalise every message, don't blast identical text, no link in a first DM to a stranger.

## Budget suggestion
At £50–200/post, a sensible test round is **3–5 partners for ~£300–600 total**: 1 trade-org mention (BSFA), 1 collective shout-out (cheap/free), and 2–3 individual trader-operators once scouted. Track results the same honest way as `GROWTH_PLAN.md` — clicks/trials per partner, not just impressions — before committing more budget to any one tier.
