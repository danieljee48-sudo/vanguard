# The Mobile Caterer's EHO-Ready Food Safety Checklist

*A one-page daily/weekly checklist covering the records an Environmental Health Officer (EHO) actually asks to see. Free — no catch. Built by the team behind VanGuard.*

> This is a practical aid, not legal advice. Always follow the FSA's **Safer Food, Better Business (SFBB)** pack and your **local authority's** guidance. UK temperature figures below follow FSA guidance for **England, Wales and Northern Ireland**. **Scotland differs** and is flagged inline: Food Standards Scotland sets **no statutory 8°C chill figure** and **no 2-hour hot-holding tolerance**, and reheating is **82°C**. If you trade in Scotland, follow **CookSafe** and Food Standards Scotland.

---

## ☀️ OPENING CHECKS (every trading day, before service)
- [ ] **Fridge/chiller temperature** logged — **8°C or below** by law in England, Wales and NI (Scotland has no statutory figure — keep food safe and follow CookSafe); aim for **5°C or below** everywhere.
- [ ] **Freezer temperature** logged — **−18°C or below**.
- [ ] Probe thermometer working + **cleaned & sanitised** (spare batteries?).
- [ ] Handwash station: hot water, soap, blue roll — stocked.
- [ ] Clean water supply full / waste water tank empty.
- [ ] Gas (LPG) checked, no smell, in-date certificate.
- [ ] Surfaces & equipment visibly clean; sanitiser made up.
- [ ] Stock in date; oldest at front (FIFO); allergen info to hand.

## 🍳 DURING SERVICE
- [ ] **Cooking core temperature 75°C+** (or 70°C for 2 min) — log a sample for each cooked item.
- [ ] **Hot holding 63°C or above.** In England, Wales and NI there is one limited exception — food may be out of temperature control for service or display for a single period of under 2 hours, once only, then it must be used or binned (log the time either way). **Scotland has no such tolerance:** treat 63°C as the line.
- [ ] Raw and ready-to-eat kept **separate** (colour boards/utensils).
- [ ] Reheating to **75°C+** (Scotland: 82°C).
- [ ] Handwashing between tasks / after handling raw / after breaks.

## 🌙 CLOSING CHECKS (end of day)
- [ ] Final **fridge/freezer temps** logged.
- [ ] Cooling: hot food cooled within **90 minutes**, then chilled.
- [ ] All surfaces, equipment & probe cleaned and sanitised.
- [ ] Waste removed; pest check (no droppings/gnaw marks).
- [ ] Stock rotated; anything out of date discarded and noted.

## 🗓️ WEEKLY
- [ ] Deep clean (extraction, hard-to-reach areas) — dated.
- [ ] Deliveries: supplier + temperature on arrival recorded.
- [ ] Allergen matrix reviewed & up to date (**Natasha's Law** for any pre-packed-for-direct-sale items).
- [ ] Review the week's records — any gaps? Fill them **now**, not at inspection.

## 📋 WHAT THE EHO ACTUALLY WANTS TO SEE
1. **Consistent, dated records** — daily temperatures, cleaning, cooking/hot-holding. Gaps are the #1 rating-killer.
2. Your **SFBB pack** completed and kept up to date.
3. **Corrective actions** — proof that when something failed (e.g. fridge too warm), you recorded what you did about it.
4. Allergen information available to customers.
5. Clean, well-maintained unit and a confident answer to "show me your records."

**The single biggest cause of a low hygiene rating for mobile caterers isn't dirt — it's missing paperwork.** Keep the records, keep the rating.
