# Trial DM & Feedback Scripts

Goal for the next 2 weeks: **10–20 caterers actually trying VanGuard.** First users AND the real UI feedback that tells us what to polish. Keep it human, short, no hard sell.

## 1. DM when someone asks for the checklist
> Hey — thanks! Here's the checklist 👇 [attach PDF/link]
>
> I actually built it because I made an app that does the whole lot on your phone (temp logs, reminders, EHO-ready PDF export). It's free for 30 days — would you be up for trying it and telling me honestly what's confusing or annoying? Your feedback would genuinely shape it. Either way the checklist's yours 🙌
> vanguardapp.co.uk

## 2. If they show interest / start the trial
> Amazing, thank you 🙏 Two quick things:
> 1. When you first open it — is anything unclear or fiddly? Tell me the truth, I want to fix it.
> 2. Log one fridge temp so you see the flow.
> I'll check in after a couple of days — no spam, promise.

## 3. Feedback follow-up (day 2–3)
> How've you found VanGuard so far? Honestly — what's the one thing that annoyed you or you couldn't figure out? That single answer is worth more to me than a five-star review 🙂

## 4. Convert to paid (near end of trial, only if they're using it)
> You've been logging with it for a few weeks now 👍 It's £7.99/mo (or £69.99/yr) after the trial — keeps the reminders, records and EHO PDF going. Want me to leave it running? And if there's anything still missing, tell me and I'll see if I can add it.

## 5. Ask for a referral (any happy user)
> Really glad it's helping. Do you know one or two other traders drowning in the paper log book? A quick "try this" from you is worth more than any advert — happy to sort them a longer free trial.

---

**Rules:** reply within a few hours while interest is warm; never argue; if someone's rude, thank them and move on; log every trial + every piece of feedback in `growth/FEEDBACK_LOG.md`.
